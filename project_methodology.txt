Methodology: The project uses a stratified 80/20 split (random_state=42), 5-fold StratifiedKFold CV on the training partition, and Scikit-learn Pipelines with a ColumnTransformer. Numerical predictors receive median imputation and StandardScaler. Categorical predictors receive most-frequent imputation and OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown='ignore'). student_id is excluded; placed is the binary target. Models are DummyClassifier, Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest. Selection is based on mean CV ROC-AUC, with F1-score as tie-break. The held-out test set is used only for final evaluation. Metrics are accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC and log loss.
